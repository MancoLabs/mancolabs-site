The rule, in plain English

In 2024, the U.S. Justice Department issued a rule under Title II of the Americans with Disabilities Act. It requires state and local government websites and mobile apps to meet WCAG 2.1 Level AA, a widely used standard for making sites usable by people with disabilities. That covers things like readable text and color contrast, pages that work with a keyboard or screen reader, labeled forms, and captions on videos.

Governments serving 50,000 people or more
April 26, 2027
Governments serving fewer than 50,000 people, and special district governments
April 26, 2028

These dates come from an extension the Justice Department published in April 2026, which moved each deadline back one year. The extension is being challenged in federal court, and the Department has said it may revisit the rule, so the dates could change.

Sources: the ADA.gov fact sheet on the rule and the April 2026 extension in the Federal Register.
