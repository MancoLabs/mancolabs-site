Email health check

Is your email set up to stay out of spam?

Type in your domain. I'll look up its public email settings and explain what they mean. It only takes a few seconds.

You can also paste an email address. The lookup uses Cloudflare's public DNS service. Nothing is stored, and no email is sent.

What it looks at

Email provider
Which company handles your email, from your mail server settings.
Sender list (SPF)
Which services are allowed to send email as your business.
Digital signature (DKIM)
A signature on each message that proves it really came from you.
Spoofing protection (DMARC)
What inboxes should do with fake email that uses your name.
